description: "Connect your OpenCode Go provider to Harness Desktop"
---

OpenCode Zen and OpenCode Go models are provided by OpenCode, not Harness. If you have an OpenCode subscription or API key, connect the `OpenCode Go` provider in the Harness desktop app.

<Note>
OpenCode Zen and OpenCode Go are subscriptions sold by OpenCode; Harness does not sell model access.
</Note>

## Connect OpenCode Go in Harness Desktop

1. Open Harness Desktop.
2. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
3. Open `Settings`.
4. Go to `AI Providers`.
5. Click `Connect`.
6. Search for `opencode`.
7. Choose `OpenCode Go`.
8. Click `Connect` and enter your OpenCode API key when prompted.

<Frame>
  ![OpenCode Go provider in the Connect providers dialog](/images/opencode-go-connect-provider.png)
</Frame>

After the provider is connected, choose an OpenCode model from the model picker in a chat.

## Manage your OpenCode account

- Learn about OpenCode Go at [opencode.ai/go](https://opencode.ai/go).
- Manage your OpenCode account or authentication at [opencode.ai/auth](https://opencode.ai/auth).

If Harness says the OpenCode key is invalid or the model is unavailable, check your OpenCode account first. Harness does not manage OpenCode Go or OpenCode Zen billing.
