## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your models" icon="microchip" href="/start-here/connect-your-stack/use-local-models">
    Ollama, vLLM, llama.cpp or LM Studio on your machine, or a hosted provider with your own key.

  <Card title="Memory" icon="brain" href="/start-here/do-work-with-it/cross-chat-memory">

## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    [Download Harness](https://github.com/vaishnavjai/harness/releases) for macOS, Windows, or Linux.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a folder">
    Harness only works in folders you authorize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a model">
    [Run a local model](/start-here/connect-your-stack/use-local-models), [add an API key](/start-here/connect-your-stack/add-anthropic-api-key), or [sign in with ChatGPT](/start-here/connect-your-stack/sign-in-with-chatgpt).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a task">
    Start with "Summarize this folder."
  </Step>
</Steps>
